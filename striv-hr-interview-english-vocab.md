# Striv HR 面试英语词汇手册

面向 **Striv HR 职位** 的面试准备材料。优先背 **官网原话**，再背通用跑步 / HR / 行政词。

STRIV 是波士顿 **Surplex, Co.** 旗下品牌，不是独立法律实体。官网定位是：把 **lab-grade movement intelligence** 放进每一次跑步，让人看清自己怎么落地、怎么滚动、怎么疲劳。口号是 **Every Step Informed**。团队自称 **engineers, scientists, designers — above all, athletes**。创始人 Axl Chen；顾问包括哈佛跑医 Dr. Adam Tenforde 和 2004 奥运选手 Kate O'Neill Tenforde。法定地址在波士顿 Harrison Avenue。

本手册分两层：

1. **官网重点（S1–S90）**：来自 [striv.run](https://striv.run/)、[striv.run/learn](https://striv.run/learn)、[用户手册](https://a.striv.run/manual.html)、About、退货与保修页。面试第一优先级。
2. **通用词（1–300）**：跑步、HR、行政的基础和进阶词，用来把官网语言嵌进人事工作。

带 ★ 的词先背。说产品时尽量用官网词，不要自己发明「250+ 传感器」「智能鞋垫 AI」这类近似说法。

**怎么用**

1. 先熟读「官网必须记住的数字和原话」，能 60 秒介绍公司。
2. 再背官网重点词 S1–S90。

4. 最后熟读文末面试段落，把官网词嵌进自己的经历。

### 0.4 Learn 页：STRIV 到底测什么

[Learn](https://striv.run/learn) 写得很清楚：Every measurement on this page is grounded in real recorded data. HR 不需要会算，但要能按官网五个模块介绍。

| 模块 | 官网标题 | 官网指标 | 中文要点 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | **Pressure underfoot** | Time to forefoot peak；pressure through **landing, midstance, push-off** | 一步里压力怎么从脚跟走到前掌 |
| 02 | **How hard you land** | Peak vertical **GRF**；estimated **VALR**；**loading time** | 你砸地有多狠、加载有多快 |
| 03 | **How your foot rolls** | **Pronation excursion**；**center of pressure trajectory** | 脚往里滚多少；压力中心走哪条路径 |
| 04 | **How your two feet compare** | L/R：**contact time, force, pronation, forefoot timing** | 左右是不是一边在补偿另一边 |
| 05 | **What changes over a run** | Loading time；peak vertical GRF | 单次平均值会掩盖后半程崩坏 |

一步的三个时相，官网反复出现，必须会说：

1. **Landing**：压力先出现在脚跟
2. **Midstance**：左右脚的 **medial / lateral** 分布可以不一样
3. **Push-off**：前掌把人推出去

Learn 页示例原话，可直接用来展示你读过科学页：

> The left keeps more pressure at heel landing and forefoot push-off, while the right shifts slightly outward at midstance. The two feet follow distinct pressure paths through the same step.

验证原话：

> Force-plate treadmills are the gold standard for measuring running forces in biomechanics labs. STRIV tracked the same changes as Bertec across GRF, contact time, duty factor, VALR, and loading time.

### 0.5 硬件与用户手册：零件和流程

手册说明这不是一块厚鞋垫，而是一套可拆的传感系统。行政、客服、HR 入职培训都会用到这些词。

**盒子里有什么 / 怎么装**

1. **Slide clip under laces** — 宽的一侧靠近脚踝
2. **Attach pod to clip** — 听到 click 才算锁上
3. **Attach battery to pod** — 电池推进去，再听一次 click
4. **Remove your current insole**
5. **Place sensors under insole** — 传感层平铺在鞋底，再把原鞋垫压回去
6. **Thread cable over laces**
7. **Connect sensor to pod**
8. **After your run: sync, then disconnect battery** — 没 sync 就拔电池，数据会丢

**关键产品规则**

- 传感垫标了 **L / R**，左右装反数据就废
- 可以 **use the sensors with your own insoles**（sensor-only kit）
- 第一次必须 **pair left and right pods**，入口叫 **Insole Control Center**
- 配好后状态从 **Not paired** 变成 **Live**
- 支持 **Run now, sync later**：校准过以后，即使蓝灯闪（没连上手机）也能记数据
- 蓝灯闪约 1 分钟后停下，表示进入 **data collection mode**
- **Blinking blue** = Bluetooth disconnected；**red to green** = calibration
- 校准要站着不动；踩上去时看 **mean pressure**，变化小于 10 可能传感器坏了
- 售后分三个表：连接/校准问题、After-Sales Service Request、Bug Report
- 传感器组合：**pressure array + IMU + barometric (elevation) + temperature**
- 连接：**Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)**
- 防水口径：**waterproof and sweatproof for normal athletic use**；不能机洗
- App 现为 **iOS TestFlight** 和 Android **Open Testing**
- 删账号：App → Personal Settings → Delete Your Account，不可撤销

> Return-to-run plans need objective data, not just “it feels okay today.”

**184. periodization** 周期化训练
> Periodization is how serious athletes organize stress and recovery over a season.

**185. VO2 max** 最大摄氧量
> VO2 max is a fitness marker, but it is not the only thing our product should optimize.

**186. lactate threshold** 乳酸阈
> Threshold work is specific, and coaching should not turn every run into a threshold run.

**187. heart rate zone** 心率区间
> Heart rate zones are familiar to users coming from Garmin or Apple Watch.

**188. GPS watch** GPS 手表
> A GPS watch already owns pace and distance; we have to win on in-shoe intelligence.

**189. IMU / 9-axis motion sensor** 惯性测量单元 / 九轴传感器
> The 9-axis sensor tracks acceleration and foot angles, which I can mention when I introduce the product.

**190. pressure mapping** 压力分布
> Pressure mapping is a lab tool we are putting into daily training.

**191. firmware** 固件
> Firmware quality affects battery, sync, and trust, so I care who we hire onto that team.

**192. battery life** ★ 续航
> The homepage says 1–2 weeks per charge; the manual says 15–20 hours of active use. Support hires need both numbers.

**193. pairing / sync** 配对 / 同步
> Pairing and sync issues will be a large share of early customer tickets.

**194. third-party integration** 第三方集成
> The official contrast is with watches and pods that estimate from the wrist or shoe. We measure under both feet.

**195. AI coaching** ★ AI 教练
> Call it Ace. It gives live cues from form, training history, and goals, and it is not a clinician.

**196. personalized insight** 个性化洞察
> Personalized insights only work if the underlying data is clean.

**197. lab-grade** ★ 实验室级
> The official phrase is “lab-grade movement intelligence on every run.” I would hire people who take that claim seriously, and who never upgrade it to clinical-grade.

**198. early-stage hardware** 早期硬件
> Early-stage hardware means ambiguity, iteration, and a lot of cross-functional work.

**199. product-market fit** ★ 产品市场契合
> I would hire for people who can live with product-market fit still being proven.

**200. wearable tech / consumer hardware** 可穿戴科技 / 消费硬件
> Consumer hardware has longer cycles than pure software, and that changes how we plan headcount.

## 三、面试可直接说的段落

下面 10 段把官网词和人事词串起来。按自己的经历改专有名词即可，不要整段背到发僵。加粗的是官网原词，优先用它们。

### 1. 开场：为什么是 Striv

> I want to work in HR at STRIV because the mission is concrete: **Every Step Informed**. You are bringing **lab-grade movement intelligence** into everyday running — a **thin sensing layer** with **256 pressure sensors, 128 per foot**, **measured underfoot, not at the waist**. Ace turns that into **live coaching cues** and scores for **form, intensity, and musculoskeletal load**. That is a much more honest **employer branding** story than “another wearable.” We are hiring people who care about **human movement** and the long game, not just shipping gadgets.

### 2. 你怎么理解这家公司的用户

> A STRIV user already has a watch for **pace** and **distance**. What they do not have is an underfoot view of **pressure, force, timing, and movement** through **landing, midstance, and push-off**. If form slips, they want an **immediate cue from Ace**, then a **run summary** that says what changed and what to focus on next. If I am talking to a hardware or support candidate, I also walk through the real product: **pair the pods**, **calibrate**, **sync before you disconnect the battery**, and never mix up **L and R**.

### 3. 你怎么做招聘

> I start every role with a **kickoff**: **must-haves**, **compensation band**, interviewers, and a target **time-to-fill**. Then I write a clear **JD**, open **outbound sourcing**, and run a **structured interview** with a **scoring rubric**. I care more about **quality of hire** than speed. At a **lean team**, one weak hire is a very expensive mistake.

### 4. 你怎么面试

> I use **behavioral** and **competency-based** questions and I push for the **STAR method**. For a hardware startup I also listen for **cross-functional** habits: Can this person work with **athletes**, engineers, and **ops** without waiting for a perfect spec? I hire for **culture add**, not just **culture fit**.

### 5. 你怎么谈薪酬

> I share the **compensation band** early. I talk about **total rewards**: **salary**, **equity**, **benefits**, and the chance to shape a category. If there is a **competing offer**, I do not panic. I ask what would make the person choose Striv besides money, and I never invent numbers I cannot defend.

### 6. 你怎么做入职

> **Onboarding** is part of recruiting. Before day one I send a **30-60-90 day plan**, assign a buddy, and finish **access control** and equipment. Week one covers the product in official language: **Every Step Informed**, **Ace**, **not a medical device**, **lab-grade not clinical-grade**, plus **confidentiality** and **PII**. I want **ramp-up time** to be short because a lean team feels every empty week.

### 7. 你怎么兼行政

> At this stage HR and **administration** are the same operating system. I can own **vendor management**, **payroll** coordination, **benefits administration**, **visitor management**, and simple **SOPs** for **onboarding** and **offboarding**. I keep just enough process: a **checklist**, an **audit trail**, and a clear **escalation** path.

### 8. 你怎么看合规

> Speed does not cancel **compliance**. I am careful with **work authorization**, **exempt / non-exempt** classification, **at-will** documentation, **background checks**, and **NDAs** before hardware demos. I treat candidate data as **PII**. The same is true for movement data. Public-facing hires must know we are **not a medical device**, the legal entity is **Surplex, Co.**, and returns need **return authorization**. The homepage’s 30-day promise and the policy’s **3-run / 30 km** cap are both things support and HR should teach the same way.

### 9. 你怎么看文化

> Culture at Striv should feel like a team of **engineers, scientists, designers, and athletes**: direct, curious, and respectful of the body. I would use **stay interviews**, manager coaching, and honest **all-hands** updates. I watch **regrettable attrition** closely. In a small company, engagement is not a survey; it is whether people still raise problems.

### 10. 收尾：你 90 天做什么

> In the first 90 days I would stabilize the hiring **pipeline**, write a practical **handbook**, set a weekly **KPI** report for leadership, and build repeatable loops for **screening**, **offers**, and **onboarding**. My **OKRs** would focus on **time-to-hire**, **offer acceptance rate**, and whether new hires can contribute without chaos. I am comfortable **wearing many hats** while we are still proving **product-market fit**.

## 五、建议背诵顺序

1. **第一天（最重要）**：第〇章全部 + 60 秒介绍。数字表、四条产品主张、Learn 五模块、团队头衔。能不看稿介绍官网。
2. **第二天**：官网重点词 S1–S90，尤其带 ★ 的。再读第 1、2、8 段口语。
3. **第三天**：通用 HR 基础 61–115 + 第 3、4、5 段。这是岗位主干。
4. **第四天**：行政 116–155 + 招聘进阶 201–230 + 手册流程（配对、校准、sync、退货）。
5. **面试前一晚**：只复习第〇章数字表、S 词带 ★ 的、第四节官网搭配。用自己的经历各说一遍。

**Every Step Informed / lab-grade movement intelligence / 256 pressure sensors, 128 per foot / underfoot, not at the waist / Ace / form, intensity, musculoskeletal load / landing–midstance–push-off / GRF / Bertec / gold-standard validated / Founding Member / Surplex, Co. / not a medical device / 30-day return / 500 miles / one-year hardware warranty**
